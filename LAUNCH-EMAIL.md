**Subject:** Start Using PEP Meetings — Record & Upload Your Meetings

Hi team,

We've launched a new tool called **PEP Meetings** that automatically transcribes and analyzes your in-person meetings. It generates summaries, identifies action items, and flags meetings that need attention — so nothing falls through the cracks.

**Here's how to get started:**

1. **Go to [your app URL]** and sign in with your school Google account
2. **Record your meetings** using the voice recorder on your phone (Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder on Android)
3. **Upload the audio file** on the app — click "New Meeting," fill in the details, and attach the recording
4. The app will automatically transcribe the conversation, identify speakers, generate a summary, and pull out action items

For shorter meetings, you can also **record directly in the app** using the Record button — no separate app needed. But for most meetings, recording on your phone and uploading afterwards is the recommended approach since it's more reliable for longer conversations.

**What to use it for:**
- Parent-teacher meetings (PTMs are coming up — let's have every meeting recorded and documented)
- Admissions meetings
- Any other in-person meetings with parents, visitors, or external folks

**One important thing — please add your team members:**
You have admin access, which means you can add the teachers and staff from your classroom. Go to **Admin → Manage Users → Add User**, enter their name and school email, and assign them to your class. When they sign in with Google, they'll automatically get access.

The goal is to have this up and running across all classrooms before the next round of PTMs.

**A note on bugs:** We've built this quickly to get it into your hands in time for PTMs, so you will likely run into some rough edges. If something breaks or doesn't work as expected, please let me know directly — a screenshot and a quick description of what happened is super helpful.

**Tips:**
- MP3, M4A, WAV, and WebM files all work — just use whatever your phone's recorder saves
- Files up to 25 MB can be uploaded directly; longer recordings work too
- After upload, the transcription takes a few minutes — you'll see the status update automatically
- You can edit speaker names, fix any transcription errors with Find & Replace, and add your own action items

Let me know if you run into any issues or have questions.
